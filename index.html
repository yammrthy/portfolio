About

Reliability that protects production

At JSW Steel, one unplanned motor failure can cost up to ₹2.5 Cr per day. A motor swap takes 4–6 hours with a spare versus 2–4 days without. My job is to make sure that day never comes.

I joined as a Graduate Engineer Trainee in July 2024 and was promoted to Assistant Manager within 12 months with a 5/5 first-year appraisal. I now run the section for electrical maintenance, managing 3 staff, 60 technicians, a ₹4 Cr annual maintenance budget and about ₹70 L per month in motor-repair vendor spend.

I combine shop-floor electrical depth (VFDs, MCC/MCCB panels, PLCs, conveyors) with digital skills (Power BI, SQL, Power Automate) to build tools that maintenance teams actually use.
